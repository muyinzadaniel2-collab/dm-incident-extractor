1. Password reset
Input: Hi, I forgot my password and the reset link isn't arriving in my email. Can you help?
Expected: Account access / Medium
Check: Correct intent, moderate urgency

2. Billing dispute
Input: I was charged $49.99 twice for my subscription this month. Please refund the duplicate payment.
Expected: Billing / Medium
Check: Detect duplicate charge and refund request

3. Feature request
Input: It would be great if your dashboard supported exporting reports to Excel.
Expected: Feature request / Low
Check: Do not classify as a bug

4. Service outage
Input: Our entire company cannot log in. All 250 employees are blocked and we have a customer demo in 20 minutes!
Expected: Technical issue / Critical
Check: Recognize business impact and urgency

5. Multiple issues
Input: My invoice is incorrect, and the mobile app keeps crashing whenever I try to update my payment method.
Expected: Billing + Technical issue
Check: Apply defined multi-intent policy

6. Vague complaint
Input: Nothing works. This is ridiculous. Fix it.
Expected: Unknown / Needs clarification
Check: Avoid inventing the cause

7. Urgent but minor
Input: URGENT!!! PLEASE FIX THIS NOW!!! The profile icon is slightly misaligned on my screen.
Expected: UI bug / Low
Check: Do not equate capitalization with severity

8. Mixed sentiment
Input: I love the product, but since yesterday every export fails with a 500 error.
Expected: Technical issue / High or Medium
Check: Prioritize the failure over positive sentiment

12. Unrelated message

13. Prompt injection

14. Forged system role

18. Production degradation
Input: API requests have been timing out for 45 minutes. Around 60% of our production traffic is failing.

Check: Recognize production impact

19. Minor cosmetic issue
Input: The footer text has a spelling mistake. Everything else works fine.
